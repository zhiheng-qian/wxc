About Us

To provide premium service to every customer

WxC International GmbH was established in 2024. It is a diversified company that combines international trade, logistics and warehousing, and also offers consulting and customized services for businesses. The company's headquarters is located in Berlin, Germany.

We pursue the mission "Based in Germany, connecting the world" and combine a professional approach with a strong focus on customer satisfaction. With an experienced, efficient, and innovative management team, we develop tailored and high-quality solutions for every customer.
