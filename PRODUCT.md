# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

German-market businesses and Chinese-speaking enterprises seeking a trusted cross-border trade, logistics, or consulting intermediary. Visitors arrive with a specific need already formed — they are not browsing; they are evaluating. Primary audience: procurement managers, founders, and operations leads at SMEs who need a reliable partner to bridge the EU–China corridor.

## Product Purpose

WxC International GmbH is a Berlin-based trade facilitation company that connects businesses across the EU–China corridor. The site exists to establish credibility and make it easy for the right people to reach out. Success means a visitor leaves confident enough to initiate contact, on their own terms and by their preferred channel.

## Positioning

The German-registered, Berlin-based bridge between European and Chinese commerce — precise, reliable, and already operating where you need us.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, precise, understated. The brand does not need to sell hard; it demonstrates competence through clarity. Three words: **exacting, grounded, bilateral**.

## Anti-references

Nothing that reads as generic "global trade" clip-art or port-and-shipping imagery. No startup warmth. No soft gradient palettes or illustrated hero sections. The site should feel closer to a firm's letterhead than a landing page template.

## Design Principles

- **Precision over decoration.** Every element earns its place; nothing is there to fill space.
- **Confidence through restraint.** The less the design shouts, the more the credentials speak.
- **Both sides of the bridge.** The three-language structure (EN / DE / 中文) is not a feature — it is the proof of concept.
- **Contact is the conversion.** There is no hard CTA. The measure of success is a visitor who knows exactly how to reach us and feels it is worth doing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. The trilingual structure requires all text to remain legible at standard body sizes across all three languages, including CJK character sets. No motion that cannot be suppressed.
